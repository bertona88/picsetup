# PicSetup Detector Claims and Validation

## Current claim level

The detector bench is a deterministic, reduced-order educational and architectural slice. It demonstrates capability-gated detector readouts and physically dimensioned signal properties. It is not calibrated instrumentation or a validated prediction of a specific commercial detector.

## Validated in this slice

Automated tests cover the eight requested detector identities, capability separation, Gaussian irradiance/map determinism, PMT photon-rate estimate, power readout, wavefront classification, Stokes-state classification, spectrum sampling, general-detector coverage, continuous-wave pulse unavailability, and malformed-input failure.

## Not validated

Browser layout still requires human mobile/desktop review. No detector response has been compared with laboratory data or a manufacturer specification. No uncertainty, noise, dynamic range, saturation, quantum efficiency, exposure, aperture, or calibration model is included.
